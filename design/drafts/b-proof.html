If something goes wrong

Who to contact, in order. You can skip straight to any step.

  1. Call the 24-hour hotlineIn your language, from arrival until you're back home.
  2. The hospital's aftercare teamMedical questions go to the doctor who treated you, including remote follow-ups after you fly home.
  3. KANIQ customer protectionA direct line for anything you're not satisfied with: service, billing, or the hospital's response.
  4. Independent mediationKorea Medical Dispute Mediation and Arbitration Agency, free of charge. We help you file.

KANIQ holds professional liability insurance of ₩100 million or more, as Korean law requires of registered agencies.
